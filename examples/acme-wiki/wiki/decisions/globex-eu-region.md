# Globex data platform runs in the EU only

**Decided:** 2026-04-13 by the Globex steering committee[^steering]

## Decision

The platform runs in an EU cloud region only.

## Context

Customer data of Globex must stay in the EU.

## Alternatives considered

A global region, cheaper; rejected.
